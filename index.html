Who we are

MCC Group is a civil construction group in Nagpur, Maharashtra, founded in 1984. It is a Class 1A registered contractor with PWD Maharashtra and has a team of more than 120 people. The group undertakes roads, bridges, canals, dams, buildings, PEB structures and related civil works in Maharashtra and Telangana.

How we work

  • Quality

    We build to the client's specification and the applicable standards. Good work is the base for everything else.

  • Time

    In infrastructure the timeline decides the result. When manpower and machinery are planned together, work does not wait, and projects finish on schedule.

  • Cost

    Finishing on time keeps manpower and machinery costs under control. That keeps a project viable for the client and for us.
