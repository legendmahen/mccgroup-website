Four decades of civil construction.

MCC Group is a civil construction group based in Nagpur, Maharashtra. It was founded in 1984 and is led by Sanjay Heda, Founder President, and Mahendra Heda, Executive Director.

What we stand for

We do quality work, complete it on time and keep the cost of the project under control. In infrastructure the timeline matters most. When manpower and machinery are planned and synchronised well, projects finish on schedule, costs stay in check and the project remains viable.

We want government departments and private parties to be able to rely on us for their civil projects. We also want the people who join us to grow professionally and stay with the company for the long term.
