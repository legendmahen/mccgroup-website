# Design notes
Palette: ink #0f1c26, concrete #eceeed, paper #f8f9f8, steel #45596a, ochre #f0b840 (accent, from excavated earth/safety yellow).
Type: Archivo variable (condensed heavy headings, regular body) + Noto Sans Devanagari. Self-hosted, font-display swap.
Layout: left-aligned; ink hero with the rock-cut photograph as the one memorable element; rows and rules instead of cards; sticky call/WhatsApp/enquiry bar on mobile.
