What MCC Group builds

Roads and bridges, canals and dams, buildings, PEB structures and the civil works that complete a project.

Roads, highways and bridges

Road and highway work includes earthwork, the compacted layers that carry traffic, drainage and the finished surface. Bridge work covers foundations, piers and the deck.

  • Roads
  • Highways
  • Bridges

Canals, dams and drainage

Canal work includes earthwork to the designed section, lining and the structures along the canal. Dams hold and release water. Storm drains in layouts carry rainwater away from plots and roads.

  • Canals
  • Canal lining
  • Dams
  • Storm drain works in layouts

Steel structures

A pre-engineered building (PEB) is a steel structure designed and fabricated as a system, then assembled on site. PEB structures and sheds suit buildings that need wide open floor space and a short build time.

Common questions

What does MCC Group build?

Roads, highways, bridges, canals, dams, commercial buildings and offices, residential buildings, schools, colleges, hospitals, shopping complexes, marriage banquet halls, PEB structures and sheds, storm drain works in layouts, landscaping and other civil works.

Where does MCC Group work?

In Maharashtra and Telangana. The head office is in Nagpur, Maharashtra.

Is MCC Group a registered contractor?

Yes. MCC Group is a Class 1A registered contractor with PWD Maharashtra.

How large is the MCC Group team?

More than 120 people.

Does MCC Group build PEB structures?

Yes. MCC Group builds PEB structures and PEB sheds.
